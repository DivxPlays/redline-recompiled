# Achievements (0.2)

New to this port: Forza Motorsport (2005) never had achievements. Each one pops up at the top of the screen when
earned (with the emblem), and **Achievements** on the main menu lists them all, earned or not. They are kept per
game profile, in `hdd\achievements\<profile name>.txt`. Progress you already had counts the first time a profile
loads: driver level, cars, credits, distance driven and Drivatar lessons.

## How they are judged

- A **win** or **podium** needs at least one other car in the race. Time Trials (you alone on the track) count only
  for Against the Clock. Split-screen races count for player 1's profile.
- **Comeback Kid:** last on the grid in a field of at least four, then first.
- **World Tour:** the sixteen locations Free Run lists from the start, in any layout. Test Track and Test Oval
  included.
- **Globetrotter:** by where the track is. North America: Maple Valley, Road Atlanta, New York, Laguna Seca, Sunset
  Speedway, Road America and the like. Europe: Silverstone, Nürburgring, Alpine. Asia: Tsukuba, Tokyo, Fujimi Kaido.
- **Ton Up / Double Ton:** held for half a second, so a bump's spike doesn't count.
- **Showroom Fresh / Gift Horse:** a car added to your garage; a gift if a reward car was just shown.
- **Wrench Turner:** credits spent at an upgrade's "purchase and install" question.
- **Fresh Coat:** a paint job saved. **Vinyl Artist:** a vinyl placed on the car (its colour step).
- **Setup Sheet:** a tuning setup saved. **Director's Cut:** a replay saved.
- **Student Teacher / Graduation Day:** the lessons your Drivatar has passed (its save, named as your profile).
- **Long Haul:** the profile's own distance driven (My Profile).
