# Redline Recompiled: its look

Recorded on 2026-11-10 by the starter kit, so new projects' looks don't repeat it.

- **Launcher direction:** *Classic* (taken; see the kit's `design/launchers.md`). Dark red and black, the headline
  ("Ready to race") with a red Play and Settings on the left, a large speedometer "R" emblem on the right, and a
  footer of links with the name and version.
- **Palette:** background `#1A0B0D` to `#060506`, accent red `#E3262E`, text `#F3EEEE` (Repo Trailers' `look`).
- **Trailer:** Repo Trailers' starter default: text cards and captioned race clips over an original beat (no
  `style` preset set).
- **Game:** `Redline Recompiled v<version>` in the bottom-right corner and the window title.
